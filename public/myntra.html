Read this first
  • Myntra's jewellery shelf is a discount shelf. Across 100 necklace, earring and set listings read in September 2026, 97 carried a discount and the median badge was 74% off. The only listing at 0% off was the only one made of real gold.
  • Stated MRPs are routinely 3x to 10x the selling price. On a read of 71 sub-500-rupee pieces, 43 printed an MRP at least 3x the selling price and 21 at least 5x. The deepest badge was 90% off a 7,860 MRP.
  • So a listing with a 0% badge is not "premium", it is unranked in every discount browse and every festival filter. That is the single most important competitor fact on this page.
  • Festive timing is live now: Big Fashion Festival opened 08 Oct 2026, with Dussehra and Diwali sales following through November.

The diagnosis in one line
  • Our catalogue is priced in the premium band but displays a 0% badge - the worst of both worlds. A premium brand with zero discount legibility reads as over-priced, not as exclusive.
  • The price point itself is not the problem. At 4,499 - 14,599 our jewellery sits exactly where Voylla and Kushal's sit, and those brands sell.
  • The problem is that nobody browsing the category ever sees us. And the three categories are not equally exposed.

Rule 2 - one reference price brand-wide
Checked 10 Oct 2026: our Myntra selling price equals the sahilondon.in MRP to the rupee on 109 shared SKUs. That is why every listing shows 0% off.
ElementDecision
Reference price (MRP)Keep the existing sahilondon.in MRP. It is already live, already credible, and already the brand's story. It is not missing - it is being used as the selling price.
Myntra selling priceRe-base it against that MRP so the badge lands in the band on Rule 1. This is the only number that moves.
What not to doDo not invent an MRP above the D2C reference price. It fixes the badge and breaks the cross-channel price story at the same time.
Segment the ladderEntry, Core, Hero and Halo tiers per category, each with its own multiple, so the catalogue has an entry price point, a volume engine and a halo anchor instead of one flat line.
Worked example at a 50% badge, jewellery: earrings 4,499 - 14,599 becomes a 2,250 - 7,300 ticket - inside Voylla's and Kushal's live band. Handbags 7,399 - 13,899 becomes 3,700 - 6,950 - mid-tier Miraggio to Hidesign entry. Both land where the category already buys.
