01 Concept — a "drop" is the unit

  • Two markets, zero mixing. China (Taobao + Xiaohongshu — Jewelry only, exposure push, Chinese titles) and International (Meta sahi_london/sahi_london_in + Shopify sahilondon.com/.in) run as separate workspaces via the market switch. Products, formats and defaults re-scope on switch — a China drop can never carry an IG format and vice versa.
  • One source, every channel. Create once; Studio re-sizes and re-crops to IG Story 9:16, IG Feed 4:5, Shopify 1:1, Taobao 1:1 and Xiaohongshu 3:4 — each with its safe spec.
  • Channel scoping is enforced, not just shown. Taobao & Xiaohongshu run Jewelry-only for now; pick a bag or tee and those destinations lock themselves out with a clear reason.
  • Hero SKUs drive the China channels — exclusively. FN303T (forget-me-not studs) and BC404 (camellia bear enamel necklace) are the Taobao & Xiaohongshu heroes: pinned on top of the product picker with real listing titles, and locked to those two channels — pick one and IG/Shopify formats drop themselves out with a reason.
  • Zero input, approval only. The week is pre-planned from traffic playbooks — XHS note formulas (单品推荐 / 送礼场景 / 避坑干货), IG's 50/30/20 mix, Reels-first reach, Tue–Thu peak slots, first-comment hashtags and UTM-tagged links. Captions come pre-written; you approve, swap or skip.
  • Everything points at one goal: site traffic. China: 种草 note → Taobao store. Intl: CTA → Shopify with utm_source on every link, so attribution is built in.
  • The product link is a first-class citizen. It is attached at the source step, not pasted later — matching how every post must ship with a link.
  • Non-designer by design. Templates, auto-crop and captions remove the "which size? which font? which file?" decisions.

02 Habits it follows

  • Primary action always reachable. Floating "New drop" sits above the thumb on every screen — Instagram-style create affordance.
  • Bottom navigation for the four jobs: decide (Home), make (Studio), schedule (Queue), reuse (Library).
  • Progress, not forms. The 3-step sheet shows dots and Back/Continue so a half-made drop is a draft, never a loss.
  • Status language you already use: Draft → Scheduled → Live.

03 Visual system

  • Warm paper + ink neutrals (never pure black/white), one ember accent used at ~10% weight so it stays loud.
  • Single typeface, Archivo 400–900 — contrast comes from weight and size, not font count. Tight, uppercase display for energy.
  • Channel colors reserved for channels (IG pink, Shopify green, Taobao orange) so brand and destination never blur.
  • Hairline rules instead of stacked cards for a calm, editorial rhythm on mobile.

04 Accessibility & craft

  • 44px+ touch targets on nav, buttons and list rows; visible focus rings.
  • Body 16px, contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for text; ember darkened on light surfaces.
  • Safe-zone overlay on preview mirrors real IG/Reels UI constraints.
  • Reduced-motion respected; layout uses transforms + opacity only.

Try the flow: New drop → product → formats → style it. The preview re-sizes live, and publishing adds a card to the Queue.
