Scale rule: a channel beating its session target by more than 30% for two straight weeks gets a proposed cadence increase. Winners are funded with time, losers are paused. That is how a zero budget still allocates capital.
Guardrails
  1. No paid ads. Ever. No boosted posts, no Promote, no sponsored placements.
  2. No GIFs anywhere in the content pipeline.
  3. No "WorkBuddy" or internal tooling names in public copy.
  4. Festival narrative in captions, never baked into pixels.
  5. Manual publish stays manual. Agents prepare; humans publish.
  6. Brand voice: "made by hand. worn with intent." closing "Sahi London. Where art meets what you wear."
  7. SKU codes in all reporting. No numeric listing IDs.
  8. Selling-floor prices never in customer-facing copy.
  9. Solemn dates respected: nothing promotional on Truth and Reconciliation Day, or before 11:00 on Remembrance Day.
  10. One market per post. Meta scope stays sahi_london and sahilondon_in.
